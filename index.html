CarWeather Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

CarWeather is a weather app for Android and Android Auto that shows live weather for wherever you currently are. This page describes what data the app handles and why.

Data we collect

CarWeather does not have a server, an account system, or any analytics or advertising integration. The app talks directly to the National Weather Service's public API (api.weather.gov) to fetch weather data for your location, and to Google Play Services to determine that location. This includes:

  • Your device's real-time location (used only to fetch and display weather for where you are — never stored anywhere except a short-lived on-device cache, never transmitted to the developer)
  • The most recently fetched weather data, cached locally on your device so the app still has something to show if a fetch fails
  • Your display preferences (temperature units, update frequency, and an optional fixed location override you can set from the phone app instead of your real location)

None of this data leaves your device except the location coordinates sent directly to the National Weather Service's API to request a forecast — the same kind of request any weather website makes, and not something the developer receives, stores, or has access to.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Location — used to fetch weather for wherever you currently are. This is required for the app's core function; there is no manual city-entry mode instead, since a car's location is never static.
  • Foreground service / background location — used only to keep weather data current while the app is actively showing on your car's display or the phone screen (for example, with the phone's screen off in a pocket or mount during a drive). This does not run when the app isn't in use.

Android Auto

When used with Android Auto, CarWeather displays the same weather data on your car's screen. No additional data is collected for the Android Auto surface beyond what's described above.

Third parties

Weather data comes from the National Weather Service (a U.S. federal government agency; see their own policies at weather.gov/privacy). Location is determined via Google Play Services, subject to Google's own privacy policy. CarWeather itself does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any other third party.

Children's privacy

CarWeather is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer through CarWeather's Google Play Store listing.
